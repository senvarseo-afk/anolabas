System Play: cost and coverage

A System ticket plays every 6-number combination from the numbers you choose. You pay for each combination; your odds per peso stay the same — you simply hold more tickets.

Crowded-combination check

Birthday-range sets (all 1–31), consecutive runs, arithmetic patterns, all-even/all-odd and 1-2-3-4-5-6 are played by many people. They are exactly as likely as any other set — but if one hits, the jackpot is split among more winners. Generated sets are flagged automatically; check your own here.

    Ultra Lotto 6/58 odds in plain numbers

    One ₱25 combination has a 1 in 40,475,358 chance at the jackpot.

    Lower tiers, multiple tickets and prizes

    There are 40,475,358 ways to choose 6 numbers from 58, so one ₱25 combination has a 1 in 40,475,358 chance at the jackpot (minimum ₱75,000,000, drawn at 9PM every Tue, Fri, Sun). Matching five of six is roughly 1 in 129,729, four of six about 1 in 2,035, and three of six about 1 in 92. Buying ten combinations gives ten chances in 40,475,358 — ten times the cost for ten times the (still tiny) chance.
