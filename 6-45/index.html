How much for 3, 4 or 5 numbers in Lotto 6/45?

In Mega Lotto 6/45, 3 numbers win ₱30 fixed; 4 numbers win a share of a ₱1,100,000 pool; 5 numbers win a share of a ₱1,200,000 pool; all 6 win the jackpot, at least ₱15,000,000. Pools are split equally among that draw's winners, so the 4- and 5-number amounts change every draw.

Claiming a prize

Up to ₱10,000 at any authorized outlet or PCSO branch; bigger prizes at PCSO, with 20% tax above ₱10,000. You have one year to claim.

Where to claim by amount, requirements and tax

PHP 10,000 and below: any authorized outlet or PCSO branch. Above PHP 10,000 up to PHP 300,000: PCSO branch office in your area or the main office (PCSO FAQ, read 2026-11-05; Citizen's Charter 2025 p. 70). Above PHP 300,000 and the jackpot: PCSO main office. Main office pays cash up to PHP 60,000 gross and by check above that (Charter 2025 pp. 40, 49). One year to claim. Two valid IDs, signed ticket, Winner's Profile Form. Prizes above PHP 10,000 are subject to 20% final tax on the full amount (TRAIN Law). Source: PCSO 2025 Citizen's Charter.
