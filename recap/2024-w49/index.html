PCSO Lotto Weekly Recap: Dec 2–8, 2024

By Marco Reyes · Updated December 8, 2024 · Independent summary of PCSO results, not affiliated with PCSO.

Between Monday, December 2, 2024 and Sunday, December 8, 2024, PCSO held 57 draws: 15 lotto draws across 5 games, plus 21 Swertres draws and 21 EZ2 draws. The biggest jackpot on offer was ₱8.9 million in Mega Lotto 6/45 on Monday, December 2. No lotto jackpot was won, so every jackpot rolled over.

Fun facts

  • Number 05 came up in five different lotto games this week (6/58, 6/55, 6/49, 6/45 and 6/42); 01, 29 and 35 also turned up in three or more games.
  • Longest-running jackpot: Ultra Lotto 6/58 had gone at least 6 straight draws without a jackpot winner as of Dec 8, with — on offer (winner data in our records starts in November 2024).
  • 10 of 15 lotto draws included at least one pair of consecutive numbers, such as 28 and 29 in Mega Lotto 6/45 on Dec 2.
  • Highest total of the six numbers: 215 in Ultra Lotto 6/58 on Dec 8. Lowest: 99 in Grand Lotto 6/55 on Dec 4.
  • Across all 15 lotto draws, 53 of the 90 numbers drawn were odd and 37 were even; no draw was all odd or all even.
