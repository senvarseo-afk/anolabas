PCSO Lotto Weekly Recap: Aug 4–10, 2025

By Marco Reyes · Updated August 10, 2025 · Independent summary of PCSO results, not affiliated with PCSO.

Between Monday, August 4, 2025 and Sunday, August 10, 2025, PCSO held 57 draws: 15 lotto draws across 5 games, plus 21 Swertres draws and 21 EZ2 draws. The biggest jackpot on offer was ₱329.8 million in Ultra Lotto 6/58 on Sunday, August 10. No lotto jackpot was won, so every jackpot rolled over.

  • Number 06 came up in five different lotto games this week (6/58, 6/55, 6/49, 6/45 and 6/42); 04, 41 and 03 also turned up in three or more games.
