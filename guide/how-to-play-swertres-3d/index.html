How to Play Swertres (3D Lotto): Price, Rambolito, Prizes and Odds

Swertres (3D Lotto) costs ₱10 per play: pick three digits from 0 to 9, choose Standard or Rambolito, and pick a draw at 2PM, 5PM or 9PM. Standard pays ₱4,500; Rambolito pays ₱1,500 or ₱750.

What is Swertres and how much does it cost?

Swertres is the everyday name for PCSO’s 3D Lotto. You pick three digits from 0 to 9 (any combination from 000 to 999), pay ₱10 per play, and choose one of three daily draws at 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM or 9:00 PM. If your three digits come out in exact order, a Standard play wins ₱4,500. PCSO renamed the game 3D Lotto, but tellers, outlets and players still say Swertres, and the rules did not change.

Swertres at 3D Lotto, iisa lang ‘yan. ₱10 kada taya, tatlong draw araw-araw.

How do I fill out a Swertres bet slip?

A Swertres slip has several panels, each with three columns of digits 0–9. Shade one digit in each column, mark the play type, mark the draw time, and hand it to the teller with your ₱10. The terminal prints a ticket; the ticket, not the slip, is your proof.

  1. Take a 3D Lotto slip (it says 3D or Swertres at the top; do not use the 6/42 or EZ2 slip).
  2. In one panel, shade one digit in the first column, one in the second, and one in the third. Example: 4 – 2 – 7 means your combination is 427.
  3. Shade Standard (exact order) or Rambolito (any order). Leave it blank and the teller will usually treat it as Standard; ask to be sure.
  4. Shade the draw time: 2PM, 5PM or 9PM. Many outlets let you shade more than one draw; each draw is a separate ₱10 play.
  5. To bet more than ₱10 on the same combination, shade the amount box (₱20, ₱50 and so on). Prizes scale in proportion.
  6. If you do not want to choose digits, shade LP (Lucky Pick) and the terminal picks for you.
  7. Pay, check that the printed ticket shows the right digits, play type, draw time and date, then sign the back.

Tandaan: ‘yung printed ticket ang mahalaga, hindi ‘yung slip. I-check mo bago umalis sa outlet.

What is the difference between Standard and Rambolito in Swertres?

Standard means your three digits must match the draw in the exact order; it pays ₱4,500 for ₱10. Rambolito means you win if your digits come out in any order, with a smaller prize because more arrangements can win. There are two Rambolito prize levels depending on whether your digits repeat.

Play typeYour digitsWinning arrangementsPrize per ₱10Chance per play
Standardany, e.g. 4-2-71 (427 only)₱4,5001 in 1,000
Rambolito 6three different, e.g. 4-2-76 (427, 472, 247, 274, 724, 742)₱7506 in 1,000
Rambolito 3two the same, e.g. 4-4-73 (447, 474, 744)₱1,5003 in 1,000

Triples such as 7-7-7 have only one arrangement, so they can be played as Standard only. The full comparison is in Standard vs Rambolito.

When are the Swertres draws?

Swertres is drawn three times every day, including Sundays, at 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM and 9:00 PM Philippine time. Betting for each draw closes shortly before draw time, so buy at least a few minutes early. PCSO may suspend draws on certain holidays (notably Maundy Thursday, Good Friday and Black Saturday); check the draw schedule and PCSO announcements.

What are the odds of winning Swertres?

With a Standard play there are 1,000 possible outcomes (000 to 999) and only one wins, so the chance is 1 in 1,000 per play. Rambolito 6 wins on 6 of the 1,000 outcomes (about 1 in 167); Rambolito 3 wins on 3 of them (about 1 in 333). Every draw is independent: the digits that came out at 2PM have no effect on 5PM, and a combination that has not appeared for months is not “due”. How do I claim a Swertres prize?

A Standard prize of ₱4,500 is ₱10,000 or below, so you can claim it at any authorized lotto outlet or a PCSO branch by presenting the original winning ticket, signed at the back, with one valid ID. No tax is deducted at this level. If you bet more than ₱10 on a combination and the total prize exceeds ₱10,000, claim at a PCSO branch; prizes above ₱10,000 are subject to a 20% final tax on the full amount (PCSO Citizen’s Charter 2025). You have one year from the draw date to claim.

Ang ₱4,500 pwede mong i-claim sa kahit anong outlet. Dalhin ang ticket na pirmado at isang valid ID.

Full steps, including the ₱10,000–₱300,000 branch bracket and the Main Office bracket above ₱300,000, are in how to claim a PCSO prize. Use the tax calculator for larger wins.
