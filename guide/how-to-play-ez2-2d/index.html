How to Play EZ2 (2D Lotto): Price, Rambolito, Prizes and Odds

EZ2 (2D Lotto) costs ₱10 per play: pick two numbers from 1 to 31, choose Standard or Rambolito, and pick a 2PM, 5PM or 9PM draw. Standard pays ₱4,000; Rambolito pays ₱2,000.

What is EZ2 and how much does it cost?

EZ2 is the popular name for PCSO’s 2D Lotto. You pick two numbers from 1 to 31, pay ₱10 per play, and choose a draw at 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM or 9:00 PM. A Standard play that matches both numbers in exact order wins ₱4,000; a Rambolito play that matches in any order wins ₱2,000. PCSO renamed the game 2D Lotto; the number range, price and prizes are unchanged, so EZ2 and 2D are the same game.

EZ2 o 2D Lotto, pareho lang. Dalawang numero mula 1 hanggang 31, ₱10 ang taya.

How do I fill out an EZ2 bet slip?

The EZ2 slip has panels with two columns, each listing 1 to 31. Shade one number in the first column and one in the second, choose Standard or Rambolito, pick the draw, and pay. The ticket printed by the terminal is your proof, so check it before you leave.

  1. Take a 2D Lotto / EZ2 slip.
  2. Shade one number from 1 to 31 in the first column and one in the second. Example: 05 in the first and 23 in the second means your combination is 05-23. The same number twice (for example 11-11) is allowed.
  3. Shade Standard (exact order) or Rambolito (any order).
  4. Shade the draw: 2PM, 5PM or 9PM. Each draw you mark is a separate ₱10 play.
  5. To stake more, shade an amount higher than ₱10; the prize increases in proportion.
  6. Shade LP if you want the terminal to choose your two numbers.
  7. Pay, read the printed ticket (numbers, play type, draw, date), and sign the back.

Should I choose Standard or Rambolito in EZ2?

Standard pays double but requires exact order; Rambolito pays ₱2,000 and wins in either order, so it has twice the chance. The expected value is the same either way; the choice is about how often you prefer to win versus how much. A pair of identical numbers such as 11-11 has only one order, so it can only be played Standard.

When are the EZ2 draws?

EZ2 is drawn every day at 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM and 9:00 PM Philippine time, alongside Swertres. Betting closes a few minutes before each draw. The 9PM draw is part of PCSO’s evening broadcast, which runs from about 9:00 to 9:30 PM, so the 9PM EZ2 numbers may appear a few minutes after nine. PCSO suspends draws on some holidays; see the full schedule.

What are the odds of winning EZ2?

There are 31 × 31 = 961 possible ordered results, so a Standard play wins 1 time in 961. A Rambolito play on two different numbers wins 2 times in 961. These odds are fixed by the game design and do not move with what came out yesterday, what a “hearing” page claims, or how long a pair has been “cold”. Our EZ2 history counts past appearances for interest only.

How do I claim an EZ2 prize?

Both EZ2 prizes (₱4,000 Standard, ₱2,000 Rambolito) are ₱10,000 or below, so you can claim at any authorized lotto outlet or PCSO branch with the original signed ticket and a valid ID, with no tax deducted. If you staked more than ₱10 and your total prize exceeds ₱10,000, you must claim at a PCSO branch and a 20% final tax applies to the full amount (PCSO Citizen’s Charter 2025). The claim deadline is one year from the draw date.

Pirmahan agad ang likod ng ticket. Isang taon ang palugit para mag-claim.

Step-by-step details, documents and brackets are in how to claim. For larger stakes, run the numbers through the tax calculator.
