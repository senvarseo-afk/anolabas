Standard vs Rambolito: Which Swertres and EZ2 Play Type Should You Choose?

Standard wins only in exact order and pays ₱4,500 (Swertres) or ₱4,000 (EZ2) per ₱10. Rambolito wins in any order and pays ₱750 or ₱1,500 (Swertres) or ₱2,000 (EZ2). Same expected value; different frequency of wins.

What is the difference between Standard and Rambolito?

Standard (also called straight or “target”) wins only if your numbers come out in the exact order drawn. Rambolito wins if your numbers come out in any order. Because Rambolito covers more arrangements, it wins more often but pays less per win. In Swertres (3D Lotto) a ₱10 Standard play pays ₱4,500; Rambolito pays ₱750 for three different digits or ₱1,500 when two digits repeat. In EZ2 (2D Lotto) Standard pays ₱4,000 and Rambolito pays ₱2,000.

Standard: eksaktong pagkakasunod. Rambolito: kahit anong ayos. ‘Yan lang ang pinagkaiba.

How does Rambolito work in Swertres?

A Swertres combination has three digits, each from 0 to 9. In Rambolito, the terminal treats your three digits as a set and pays if the draw is any permutation of that set. The number of permutations depends on whether digits repeat, and the prize is divided accordingly.

The prize is set so that each covered arrangement is worth ₱750 of the ₱4,500 Standard prize (₱4,500 ÷ 6) or ₱1,500 (₱4,500 ÷ 3). Full game rules: how to play Swertres.

How does Rambolito work in EZ2?

EZ2 has two numbers from 1 to 31. A Rambolito play on two different numbers, say 07 and 19, wins if the draw is 07-19 or 19-07, and pays ₱2,000 per ₱10, half the ₱4,000 Standard prize. A pair of identical numbers such as 19-19 has only one order, so it can only be played Standard.

Which pays more over time, Standard or Rambolito?

Neither. The expected return per ₱10 is the same because the prize is divided by exactly the number of arrangements covered. Over 1,000 Swertres plays you would expect one Standard win of ₱4,500, or six Rambolito 6 wins of ₱750 each, also ₱4,500. The difference is the pattern: Rambolito gives smaller, more frequent wins; Standard gives rarer, larger ones. Pick the pattern you are comfortable with, and remember that both lose money on average, since ₱4,500 back per ₱10,000 staked is a 45% return.

Pareho lang ang average. Rambolito: madalas pero maliit. Standard: bihira pero malaki. Pareho ring talo sa katagalan.

How do I mark Rambolito on the bet slip?

Both the Swertres and EZ2 slips have a box for the play type next to each panel.

  1. Shade your digits (three columns for Swertres, two for EZ2).
  2. Shade Rambolito in the play-type box. Leave it blank and most terminals default to Standard, so check the printed ticket.
  3. Shade the draw time (2PM, 5PM, 9PM) and the amount if more than ₱10.
  4. Read the ticket: it will say “RAMBOLITO” or “R” and show the prize type before you pay.

If you want both, you can play the same digits twice, once Standard and once Rambolito, on two panels for ₱20 total. If the exact order hits, both panels win.

Does Rambolito change my odds of winning something?

Yes, it raises the chance of a win and lowers the prize in the same proportion. For three different digits your chance per Swertres play goes from 1 in 1,000 to 6 in 1,000; for two different EZ2 numbers it goes from 1 in 961 to 2 in 961. What it does not do is improve the odds of the underlying digits appearing. No play type, “hearing”, or pattern can do that; each draw is independent. See Swertres hearing explained for why.

How do I claim a Rambolito prize?

All Rambolito prizes at the ₱10 stake (₱750, ₱1,500, ₱2,000) are below ₱10,000, so claim them at any authorized lotto outlet or a PCSO branch with the signed original ticket and a valid ID, with no tax deducted, within one year of the draw (PCSO Citizen’s Charter 2025). If you staked enough that the prize exceeds ₱10,000, a PCSO branch pays it in cash after the 20% final tax. Steps: how to claim.
