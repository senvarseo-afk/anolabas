PCSO Lotto Draw Schedule: What Time Is the Draw and Where to Watch

Swertres and EZ2 draw daily at 2PM, 5PM and 9PM. The six-number games draw at 9PM on fixed days: 6/42 Tue/Thu/Sat, 6/45 Mon/Wed/Fri, 6/49 Tue/Thu/Sun, 6/55 Mon/Wed/Sat, 6/58 Tue/Fri/Sun. Watch live on PCSO's Facebook hub or IBC-13.

What time is the PCSO lotto draw?

PCSO holds draws at 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM and 9:00 PM Philippine time. Swertres (3D) and EZ2 (2D) are drawn at all three times every day. The six-number games (6/42, 6/45, 6/49, 6/55, 6/58) and the 4D and 6D digit games are drawn only at 9:00 PM, each on its own three days of the week. The 9PM draw is a live broadcast window that runs from about 9:00 to 9:30 PM, so each game’s numbers come out at a slightly different minute.

2PM, 5PM, 9PM ang Swertres at EZ2 araw-araw. Ang 6-number lotto, 9PM lang sa nakatakdang araw.

An easy way to remember the six-number games: Monday 6/45 and 6/55; Tuesday 6/42, 6/49 and 6/58; Wednesday 6/45 and 6/55; Thursday 6/42 and 6/49; Friday 6/45 and 6/58; Saturday 6/42 and 6/55; Sunday 6/49 and 6/58. The schedule page shows which games draw tonight.

Why do 9PM results come out at different times?

Because the 9PM draw is one broadcast with several games drawn in sequence. PCSO’s own production specification describes a window of roughly 9:00 to 9:30 PM, with the digit games and the six-number games taking turns on air. That is why a Swertres 9PM result may be known at 9:05 while the 6/58 numbers appear closer to 9:20. On this site each 9PM result is stamped with the time it was verified against PCSO-hosted records, not a generic “9PM”.

Hindi sabay-sabay lumalabas ang 9PM results. Normal lang na mauna ang Swertres at mahuli ang 6/58.

Where can I watch the draw live?

PCSO streams the draws on its official Facebook page, PCSO Games Online Hub, and the 9PM draw is also televised on IBC-13 and its digital channel D8TV. The stream is the fastest public source, usually a few minutes ahead of the written results. After the draw, PCSO posts the official numbers on its website, and we match our results to PCSO-hosted draw records before marking them “Verified vs PCSO” (see how we verify).

  1. Open the PCSO Games Online Hub page on Facebook shortly before 2PM, 5PM or 9PM.
  2. Watch the live video; the numbers are shown on screen as the balls are drawn.
  3. Confirm against our results pages, which carry a verification label, or PCSO’s own posted results.
  4. Treat any “result” posted before the live draw as fake. Real numbers cannot exist before the balls drop.

What time do outlets stop selling before a draw?

Outlets stop accepting bets for a draw a short time before it starts, typically several minutes before 2PM, 5PM and 9PM, when the terminal closes the draw. Exact cut-off can vary slightly by outlet, so buy early. Bets placed after the cut-off are registered for the next draw, and the draw date and time are printed on the ticket; check them before you leave.

Are draws held on holidays?

Most holidays have normal draws, but PCSO suspends draws on certain days, most consistently Maundy Thursday, Good Friday and Black Saturday, and occasionally on Christmas Day, New Year’s Day or during declared calamities and nationwide emergencies. Suspensions are announced on PCSO’s official channels, usually days ahead, and the affected game simply resumes on its next regular draw day; jackpots carry over. When a draw is suspended we show no result for that date rather than a guess, and we note it on the game’s history page.

Semana Santa: walang draw tuwing Huwebes Santo, Biyernes Santo at Sabado de Gloria. Ibang holidays, tingnan ang anunsyo.

How soon are results posted here?

For each draw we publish the numbers as soon as they are available, marked Provisional during the live window if PCSO’s record is not yet online, then Verified vs PCSO once matched. Each game’s page, for example Swertres or 6/58, shows the latest draw at the top with its status and time. Past draws are in history, and any ticket can be checked against any date with the ticket checker.
