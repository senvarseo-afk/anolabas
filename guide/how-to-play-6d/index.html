How to Play 6D Lotto: Price, Prizes, Odds and Rollover

6D Lotto costs ₱10 per combination: pick one digit in each of six columns for the 9PM draw on Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday. First prize starts at ₱150,000 and lower prizes pay from ₱40 to ₱40,000.

What is 6D Lotto and how much does it cost?

6D is PCSO’s six-digit game, which PCSO describes as the active form of the traditional sweepstakes. You pick one digit from 0 to 9 in each of six columns and pay ₱10 per combination, tax included. The draw is at 9:00 PM every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, and it can be played in Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao.

Anim na digit, ₱10 ang taya. Martes, Huwebes at Sabado, 9PM ang bola.

What are the 6D prizes?

There are five prize levels, and every level needs exact order. The lower prizes count from either end of the number. All amounts are per ₱10 played.

PrizeHow to winAmount per ₱10
1st prizeAll 6 digitsPool, ₱150,000 minimum
2nd prizeFirst 5 or last 5 digits₱40,000
3rd prizeFirst 4 or last 4 digits₱4,000
4th prizeFirst 3 or last 3 digits₱400
5th prizeFirst 2 or last 2 digits₱40

Suppose the winning number is 8-7-3-2-8-9. A ticket with 873205 wins 3rd prize for the first four digits, 503289 also wins 3rd prize for the last four, and 110089 wins 5th prize for the last two. A ticket with 138280 wins nothing, even though digits in the middle match, because those digits are not at either end.

How does the 6D first prize roll over?

The first prize starts at ₱150,000. When nobody wins, PCSO carries the pool forward to the next draw, which is why the amount on the 6D results page can climb for weeks. When there is more than one winner, the pool is divided in proportion to their bets, and each winner is guaranteed ₱150,000 for each ₱10 bet.

PCSO notes that the first prize is pari-mutuel, so a winner can receive less than the published amount, depending on total sales and the number of winners.

How do I fill out a 6D bet slip?

  1. Take a 6D slip.
  2. Shade one digit from 0 to 9 in each of the six columns, or tell the teller your numbers. You can also ask for Lucky Pick.
  3. Mark your stake in the AMT column. The minimum is ₱10 per combination, and you may play more.
  4. To repeat the numbers, mark 2 to 6 consecutive draws in the DRAW panel.
  5. If you make a mistake, mark VOID to cancel that board.
  6. Pay, read the printed ticket, and sign the back.

What are the odds in 6D?

There are 1,000,000 possible results, so one combination has a 1 in 1,000,000 chance at the first prize. Because the lower prizes count from either end, a two-digit match comes up roughly twice in 100 draws for any one ticket. Every draw is independent, and past results do not change these odds.

How do I claim a 6D prize?

The ₱40, ₱400 and ₱4,000 prizes can be claimed at any authorized lotto outlet at the minimum stake. The ₱40,000 prize and the first prize are above ₱10,000, so they are taxed 20% on the whole amount and are claimed at a PCSO office. A ₱40,000 prize pays ₱32,000 after tax. Bring the signed ticket and valid IDs within one year of the draw.

Details are in how to claim, and the tax calculator works out any amount.

Where do I see today’s 6D result?

Each draw is posted on the 6D results page with a verification label against PCSO-hosted records. The ticket checker tests a ticket against any date, and the 6D history lists past draws by month.
