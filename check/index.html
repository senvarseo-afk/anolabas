How to check a lotto ticket

  1. Pick the game printed on your ticket.
  2. Pick the draw date (and 2PM, 5PM or 9PM for Swertres and EZ2).
  3. Type your numbers in the order on the ticket.
  4. Tap Check to see matches, prize tier and take-home after tax.
  5. If you won, sign the back and read the claiming guide.
Tips: older dates, several draws, System Play tickets

The game list covers Swertres (3D Lotto), EZ2 (2D Lotto), 4D, 6D, Lotto 6/42, Mega Lotto 6/45, Super Lotto 6/49, Grand Lotto 6/55 and Ultra Lotto 6/58. The date list defaults to the latest draw; choose "Older date…" for anything further back. For Swertres and EZ2 you can also choose "All draws that day" if you bet the same numbers on every draw.

Use "Add another ticket" if you have several combinations or a System Play ticket (enter each six-number line as its own ticket). Each ticket gets its own result: numbers matched, the prize tier, the amount where PCSO publishes a fixed figure, and the estimated take-home after tax. Keep a winning ticket safe before going to the outlet or PCSO branch.

Standard vs Rambolito

Standard must match in exact order (Swertres ₱4,500, EZ2 ₱4,000 per ₱10); Rambolito wins in any order for a smaller prize.

Rambolito 3 vs 6, EZ2 and six-number games

Standard is also called "straight" or "target", and Rambolito "rumble". In Swertres a combination with a double digit such as 1-1-5 has three possible arrangements, so Rambolito 3 pays ₱1,500; a combination with three different digits such as 1-5-9 has six arrangements, so Rambolito 6 pays ₱750. Triple digits (7-7-7) cannot be played Rambolito. In EZ2, Rambolito on two different numbers pays ₱2,000. The checker applies these rules automatically when you select the bet type.

For the six-number lotto games the order never matters: match all six to win the jackpot, and three, four or five matches win lower-tier prizes. Three matches pay PCSO's fixed prize, shown with your result. Five and four matches share a prize pool equally per standard bet, so the checker shows the tier and the pool instead of guessing your share.

How are 4D and 6D tickets checked?

Enter the digits in the order on your ticket. Only exact order counts: 4D pays from the end of the number, 6D from either end.

4D Perm, Roll and why the first prize has no exact amount

A 4D ticket wins ₱800 when the last three digits match and ₱100 when the last two match, per ₱10 played. A 6D ticket wins ₱40,000, ₱4,000, ₱400 or ₱40 per ₱10 when the first or last five, four, three or two digits match. Matching digits in the middle pays nothing.

Perm (rambol) and Roll buy several straight combinations, so choose the play type and enter the numbers you marked. The checker tests every combination and adds up the fixed prizes, using the amount played per combination.

The first prize is a pool shared by its winners, and we do not know what the other winners bet. The checker shows PCSO's guaranteed minimum with the pool and winner count, and only PCSO validation gives the final amount. The 4D guide and 6D guide have the full prize tables.

Tax on lotto prizes

Prizes above ₱10,000 pay 20% tax on the whole amount; ₱10,000 and below are paid in full.

Examples and the legal basis

Prizes above PHP 10,000 are subject to 20% final tax on the full amount (TRAIN Law). Source: PCSO 2025 Citizen's Charter. The tax is applied to the whole prize, not only to the part above ₱10,000, so a ₱20,000 prize nets ₱16,000 while a ₱10,000 prize nets the full ₱10,000. Swertres and EZ2 prizes are all ₱10,000 or less on a ₱10 play and are paid in full. Use the tax calculator for any amount.

Claiming your prize

Up to ₱10,000 at any authorized outlet; bigger prizes at a PCSO branch or the main office, within one year of the draw.

Where to claim by amount and what to bring

PHP 10,000 and below: any authorized outlet or PCSO branch. Above PHP 10,000 up to PHP 300,000: PCSO branch office in your area or the main office (PCSO FAQ, read 2026-11-05; Citizen's Charter 2025 p. 70). Above PHP 300,000 and the jackpot: PCSO main office. Main office pays cash up to PHP 60,000 gross and by check above that (Charter 2025 pp. 40, 49). One year to claim. Two valid IDs, signed ticket, Winner's Profile Form. Winning tickets are valid for one year from the draw date; after that the prize is forfeited. Step-by-step instructions, documents and office hours are in the how-to-claim guide.

Ticket checker FAQ

Is this checker official?

No. LottoPH Today is an independent site and is not connected to PCSO. Results are verified against PCSO-hosted draw records, but only validation of the physical ticket at an authorized outlet or PCSO office is final.

Why is there no exact amount for 4 or 5 matches?

Those tiers share a prize pool equally among every winning standard bet, so the amount depends on how many tickets won that draw. The checker shows your tier and the pool; PCSO's validation gives the final figure. The fixed 3-of-6 prize is shown as an amount, taken from PCSO's current prize table, so very old draws may differ.

Can I check an old ticket?

Yes, as long as the draw is in our archive (most games back to 2022). Choose "Older date…" and pick the date. Remember that prizes can only be claimed within one year of the draw.

How do I check a System Play ticket?

A System ticket is several six-number combinations on one slip: System 7 is 7 combinations, System 8 is 28. Enter each combination as its own ticket and add up the prizes, because PCSO pays every winning combination on the slip, not only the highest one. For bigger System tickets use the payout chart on PCSO's game page or have the outlet validate the ticket; this checker does not expand System tickets for you. See System Play explained.

Does the checker store my numbers?

No. Nothing is sent to a server; the check runs in your browser. The simulator can keep a "my numbers" list on your own phone if you choose to save one.
